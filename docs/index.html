Event Calendar NG

Your day, one click from the clock. Calendar, agenda, tasks, meetings and weather in the Plasma panel: see what's next at a glance, join a call in one click, and never miss a meeting again.

Features

Every view you need

Month, Week, Day and Agenda. The Day view puts your hours, a small month, what's up next, today's tasks and the weather side by side.

Never late again

The clock shows your next meeting and a progress bar while it runs; an optional full-screen alert with one big Join button when it starts.

One-click Join

Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, Webex, Slack huddles and about 35 more, from the agenda, the clock or the notification.

Your tasks too

Google Tasks and TickTick in their own tab: smart lists, priorities, subtasks, quick add in plain sentences.

All your calendars

Several Google accounts, CalDAV (Nextcloud, Fastmail, iCloud…), KDE PIM (Akonadi), holidays and .ics files; an event in two calendars shows once.

Fast and offline-ready

Opens instantly with your last events and tasks, even without a network, and stays quick with busy calendars.

At home in Plasma

Rounded cards (solid or translucent) or a flat look, light and dark themes, translated into 20 languages.

Private by design

Sign-ins stay in your system keyring; no tracking, no servers of ours in between.

Install

From the KDE Store

Right click the panel > Add or Manage Widgets > Get New Widgets > Download New Plasma Widgets, search for Event Calendar NG, or get it from store.kde.org.

From a release

Download the .plasmoid file from the latest release, then:

kpackagetool6 -t Plasma/Applet -i eventcalendar-ng-*.plasmoid

To upgrade an installed Event Calendar, use -u instead of -i, then restart Plasma.

From source

git clone https://github.com/divinae/plasma-applet-eventcalendar.git eventcalendar
cd eventcalendar
sh ./install

sh ./update pulls and reinstalls; sh ./uninstall removes the widget.

Setup

  1. Add Event Calendar NG to a panel, or replace the Digital Clock with it.
  2. Right click it > Configure > Calendars: sign in with Google, add a CalDAV account or an .ics calendar.
  3. Weather: search for your city (Open-Meteo, no key needed).

How it works

This runs only in your browser: the address is read here, nothing is sent anywhere.

Steps
  1. Sign in from the widget's settings (Calendars > Google Calendar).
  2. Copy the whole address of the page you landed on.
  3. Paste it on the left, then copy the code into the widget.
HTTP 400 error?
  • Make sure you copied the whole address.
  • With your own client id, its redirect address must match exactly.

Privacy

The widget talks only to the services you set up (Google, your CalDAV server, the weather service you choose…), straight from your computer. There are no servers of ours in between and no tracking. Sign-ins and passwords stay in your system keyring. Details in the Privacy Policy.
